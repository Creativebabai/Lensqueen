Meet The Photographer

Where Hospitality Meets My Lens

I am Lensqueen — an independent luxury hotel and hospitality photographer based in Visakhapatnam. With over 4 years of creative partnership with Novotel, I specialize in translating architectural grandeur, resort atmosphere, and culinary artistry into compelling, brand-consistent visual stories.

To me, hotel photography is about evoking a guest's desire to arrive. It is catching the morning ocean light sweeping across an executive suite, the geometric symmetry of your grand lobby, or the steam rising from a chef's signature plate. Working with me means you work directly with the artist behind the camera — from lighting and framing to personal raw image curation — ensuring every asset honors your brand guidelines flawlessly.

✦

A Photographer's Note: For a true view, I upload raw, uncompressed images — preserving every nuance of natural light, rich marble textures, and food colors exactly as my camera captured them, free from artificial filtering.

My Promise

Unwavering Brand Consistency

Every photograph. Every frame. Every story. As your dedicated photographer, I treat your hotel's visual language as sacred — adhering strictly to your brand guidelines across every campaign, season, and marketing touchpoint.

01

Cohesive Visual Identity

I maintain your hotel's unique color palette, lighting mood, and aesthetic across all photos and videos — ensuring every asset feels part of one unified, prestigious brand universe.

02

Strict Brand Manual Adherence

I study and respect your brand standards meticulously — from room dressing and architectural angles to culinary presentation and guest ambiance codes on every shoot.

03

Raw Quality & Transparency

Every shoot undergoes my personal quality standards. I deliver raw, uncompressed files so your marketing team has maximum grading latitude and authentic clarity.

04

Dedicated Creative Partnership

Reflected in my 4+ year creative journey with Novotel, I build lasting relationships — becoming your hotel's trusted visual partner who knows every angle and lighting hour of your property.
